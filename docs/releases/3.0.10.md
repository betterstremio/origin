Origin no longer keeps playing when you switch away from it. Settings take on the look of the new Home, phones get the Shows, Movies, Anime and Catalogue tabs, and tablets get their own layout.

**What’s new**

- When Origin goes to the back (another app in front, the LG Home launcher or quick settings over it, a minimised window, the phone's home screen, a locked screen), background trailers stop, a film pauses and the app stops drawing. Before, a trailer could keep playing with sound over YouTube, and the app kept using the TV's processor. The film stays paused when you come back; press Play to carry on. This is on every platform (#10).
- Settings use the same look as Home: a light highlight, plain switches, no coloured accent or see-through panel.
- Phones: Shows, Movies, Anime and Catalogue are a row of buttons under Home's title. Anime and Catalogue show when they are on in Settings. The bottom bar is now Home, Search and My List, and Discover is gone, as on the TV since 3.0.0.
- Tablets: a floating bar at the top with Search, the tabs, My List and your profile picture, and a full-width picture at the top of Home and each tab that moves to the next title every few seconds. Swipe it sideways to change titles, tap it to open one.
- Phones and tablets keep the top of every page clear of the status bar and the camera cutout.

**Opening the Mac app the first time**

Origin is not signed with an Apple Developer ID. Drag it to Applications, then run this in Terminal before you open it:

```
xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Origin.app
```

On macOS 26, Open Anyway in System Settings can leave Origin bouncing in the Dock with no window. If that happened, force quit it (Option-Command-Esc), run the command, and open it again. Run the command again after each update.

**Which file to download**

- LG webOS uses `io.github.betterstremio.origin_3.0.10_arm.ipk` or the Homebrew Channel.
- Android TV, Google TV and most TV boxes use `io.github.betterstremio.origin_3.0.10_android-arm64.apk`.
- Fire TV sticks and older boxes use `io.github.betterstremio.origin_3.0.10_android-armv7.apk`. The `_android.apk` includes both CPU types.
- Android phones and tablets use `io.github.betterstremio.origin_3.0.10_android-phone-beta.apk`.
- Windows uses `io.github.betterstremio.origin_3.0.10_windows_setup.exe` or the portable zip.
- Macs with Apple Silicon (beta) use `io.github.betterstremio.origin_3.0.10_macos-arm64.dmg`.
- Samsung TVs (preview) use `io.github.betterstremio.origin_3.0.10_tizen-beta.tpk`.
