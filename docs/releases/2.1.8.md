Addons with long setup links now work, and Nuvio profiles show their pictures.

**Which file to download**
- Android phones and tablets: `tv.stremioorigin.app_2.1.8_android-phone-beta.apk`
- Android TV, Google TV and most TV boxes: `tv.stremioorigin.app_2.1.8_android-arm64.apk`
- Fire TV sticks and older boxes: `tv.stremioorigin.app_2.1.8_android-armv7.apk` (`_android.apk` holds both CPU types)
- LG webOS: `tv.stremioorigin.app_2.1.8_arm.ipk`
- Windows: `tv.stremioorigin.app_2.1.8_windows_setup.exe`, or the portable zip

**Fixes**
- Meteor said "No debrid service is configured for this install" even with a debrid key set (#5). Meteor keeps your whole setup in its install link, and links over 512 characters were cut short, so the key never reached it. Links up to 2048 characters now go through whole.
- With a Nuvio account, an addon with a long link could be missing from the list altogether. The same cut caused it, and the longer limit fixes it too.
- An addon link with a `?` part (for example `manifest.json?token=...`) now keeps it on every request, as Stremio does.

**Nuvio profiles**
- "Who's watching?" and the profile circle in the top bar show the picture you picked in Nuvio. Profiles without one keep their colour and initial. Pictures from Nuvio's supporter set don't show yet.
