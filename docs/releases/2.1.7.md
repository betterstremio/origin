Origin now runs on Android phones and tablets, as a beta.

**Which file to download**
- Android phones and tablets: `tv.stremioorigin.app_2.1.7_android-phone-beta.apk`
- Android TV, Google TV and most TV boxes: `tv.stremioorigin.app_2.1.7_android-arm64.apk`
- Fire TV sticks and older boxes: `tv.stremioorigin.app_2.1.7_android-armv7.apk` (`_android.apk` holds both CPU types)
- LG webOS: `tv.stremioorigin.app_2.1.7_arm.ipk`
- Windows: `tv.stremioorigin.app_2.1.7_windows_setup.exe`, or the portable zip

**Phones and tablets (beta)**
- The phone file is the same app as the TV one. On a phone it opens upright with its own touch screens.
- Home and Discover show all their rows; swipe a row sideways. Tap a title to open it, or a Continue Watching card to pick up where you left off. Hold a title to open a bigger card that plays its preview.
- Title pages have Play, the streams list, seasons and episodes. Search uses the phone's keyboard. Library, Settings and Nuvio profiles have phone layouts too.
- Films turn the phone sideways and fill the screen. Tap to show the controls. Double-tap the left or right side to skip 10 seconds, or drag the bar. The film pauses when you leave the app.
- Sign in with your browser on the same phone, or with your email.
- Missing so far: reviews, More like this, right-to-left languages, and translations of the new phone text. It has been tested on few phones, so please report anything that looks wrong or feels slow.
