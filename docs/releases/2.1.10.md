Rows on Home now move the other way round: the open card stays at the start of the row and the row slides under it.

**Which file to download**
- Android phones and tablets: `tv.stremioorigin.app_2.1.10_android-phone-beta.apk`
- Android TV, Google TV and most TV boxes: `tv.stremioorigin.app_2.1.10_android-arm64.apk`
- Fire TV sticks and older boxes: `tv.stremioorigin.app_2.1.10_android-armv7.apk` (`_android.apk` holds both CPU types)
- LG webOS: `tv.stremioorigin.app_2.1.10_arm.ipk`
- Windows: `tv.stremioorigin.app_2.1.10_windows_setup.exe`, or the portable zip

**Home**
- In Home's poster rows, the first place is an open card from the moment you reach the row. Left and Right slide the row under it, and the open card changes to the next title as the row moves. Up and Down land on an open card too.
- Poster cards now show part of the title's wide picture, with its logo, so the open card is the same picture made wider. The cut sits on the people in the picture. A title with no wide picture keeps its poster.
- Other rows also keep the selected card at the start and slide under it.
- The shine that passed over the selected card is gone.

**Speed**
- A card first shows a small picture and turns sharp a moment later, once nothing else is loading. Rows fill as fast as in 2.1.9, including on a Fire TV-class device (1 GB of memory, 2 cores; tested on an emulator).

**Mouse and Magic Remote**
- Pointing at a card no longer moves the row. The round arrows at a row's ends move it a page, now on LG TVs with the Magic Remote as well as on Windows. In Home's rows, click a card to bring it into the open place.
