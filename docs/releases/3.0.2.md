The Anime tab now works like Shows: proper artwork with the show's logo, trailers in the open card, one card per show and one page with every season.

**Which file to download**
- Android phones and tablets: `io.github.betterstremio.origin_3.0.2_android-phone-beta.apk`
- Android TV, Google TV and most TV boxes: `io.github.betterstremio.origin_3.0.2_android-arm64.apk`
- Fire TV sticks and older boxes: `io.github.betterstremio.origin_3.0.2_android-armv7.apk` (`_android.apk` holds both CPU types)
- LG webOS: `io.github.betterstremio.origin_3.0.2_arm.ipk`
- Windows: `io.github.betterstremio.origin_3.0.2_windows_setup.exe`, or the portable zip
- Samsung TVs (preview): `io.github.betterstremio.origin_3.0.2_tizen-beta.tpk`

**Anime**
- AniList still decides what goes in each row, but each title is now matched to its IMDb entry, so its card is drawn the same way as on Shows: a wide picture cut to the card with the show's logo at the bottom. Before, the cards were AniList's own covers, and the name only appeared after a separate lookup for each title, sometimes many seconds later.
- Trailers play in the open card and the billboard, using the same IMDb trailers Shows uses. Before, most anime cards never played one.
- AniList lists every season separately, so a row could show Attack on Titan five times. Seasons of the same show now share one card.
- Opening an anime shows one page with every season, its cast and its IMDb details, like any other series. Your stream addons are asked for it the same way as for Shows.
- If you have already watched an anime through Anime Kitsu, it keeps its Anime Kitsu page, so your progress and watched episodes stay where they are.
- The few titles with no IMDb match keep their AniList cover, and now show their name straight away.
- The matches come from arm.haglund.dev. Origin asks for a whole row at once, the first time you see it, and keeps the answers on the device. Later visits only ask about titles it has not seen before.

**Long series**
- Marking episodes watched on a series with more than 1,024 episodes, such as One Piece, could corrupt the app's memory. This is fixed.
