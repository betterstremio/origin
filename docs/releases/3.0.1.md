Fixes the TV's screen saver coming up during a film, and stops the picture cache from filling the TV.

**Which file to download**
- Android phones and tablets: `io.github.betterstremio.origin_3.0.1_android-phone-beta.apk`
- Android TV, Google TV and most TV boxes: `io.github.betterstremio.origin_3.0.1_android-arm64.apk`
- Fire TV sticks and older boxes: `io.github.betterstremio.origin_3.0.1_android-armv7.apk` (`_android.apk` holds both CPU types)
- LG webOS: `io.github.betterstremio.origin_3.0.1_arm.ipk`
- Windows: `io.github.betterstremio.origin_3.0.1_windows_setup.exe`, or the portable zip
- Samsung TVs (preview): `io.github.betterstremio.origin_3.0.1_tizen-beta.tpk`

**Screen saver during a film (LG)**
- About two minutes after the last button press, an LG TV asks every app whether its screen saver may start. Origin is meant to say no while a film plays, but it never recognised the question: the TV writes it with spaces Origin did not expect. With no answer, the TV started the screen saver anyway, every few minutes.
- Origin now reads the question as the TV sends it and says no while a film plays. When the film is paused or ended, the screen saver comes as usual.

**Picture cache**
- The cache of posters and pictures had a limit, but Origin only enforced it when it started. On an LG C1 it reached about 490 MB in one evening, and the TV had too little space left to install an update.
- Origin now keeps the cache under its limit while it runs, and it always leaves at least 150 MB free for the TV and its other apps.
- If space is short when you install an update, Origin empties its picture cache first. Pictures load from the internet again once and are cached as before.
