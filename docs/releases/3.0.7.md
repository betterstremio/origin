Origin adds Brazilian Portuguese and improves Continue Watching, stream selection, and subtitle add-on controls. [Issue #12](https://github.com/betterstremio/origin/issues/12)

**What’s new**

- Choose **Português (Brasil)** under **Settings → Language**. Origin also recognizes the Brazilian Portuguese system locale.
- **Continue Watching** sorts by most recent playback. Hold OK on a title to remove it from the row on this device. Its saved position and My List entry are kept, and watching it again brings it back.
- Stream lists mark known cached sources with a lightning bolt and known uncached sources with an hourglass. In the player, **Sources** opens on the stream you’re watching when it becomes available.
- **Settings → Subtitles** adds a preferred subtitle add-on and a switch for the built-in OpenSubtitles v3. Subtitle add-on requests allow more time and can recover the video filename and size when the stream add-on leaves them out.

**Which file to download**

- LG webOS uses `io.github.betterstremio.origin_3.0.7_arm.ipk` or the Homebrew Channel.
- Android TV, Google TV and most TV boxes use `io.github.betterstremio.origin_3.0.7_android-arm64.apk`.
- Fire TV sticks and older boxes use `io.github.betterstremio.origin_3.0.7_android-armv7.apk`. The `_android.apk` includes both CPU types.
- Android phones and tablets use `io.github.betterstremio.origin_3.0.7_android-phone-beta.apk`.
- Windows uses `io.github.betterstremio.origin_3.0.7_windows_setup.exe` or the portable zip.
- Samsung TVs (preview) use `io.github.betterstremio.origin_3.0.7_tizen-beta.tpk`.
- The Mac beta is available from the [3.0.2 release](https://github.com/betterstremio/origin/releases/tag/v3.0.2).
