Files with DTS or TrueHD sound now play through on LG TVs without stopping or buffering, and subtitles get softer colours.

**Which file to download**
- Android phones and tablets: `tv.stremioorigin.app_2.1.9_android-phone-beta.apk`
- Android TV, Google TV and most TV boxes: `tv.stremioorigin.app_2.1.9_android-arm64.apk`
- Fire TV sticks and older boxes: `tv.stremioorigin.app_2.1.9_android-armv7.apk` (`_android.apk` holds both CPU types)
- LG webOS: `tv.stremioorigin.app_2.1.9_arm.ipk`
- Windows: `tv.stremioorigin.app_2.1.9_windows_setup.exe`, or the portable zip

**Fixes**
- On LG TVs, a film whose DTS or TrueHD sound is converted on the TV could stop partway and bring up the screensaver (#7). The converter asked the addon for the file again on every small read, and when one of those requests failed it could take the film as finished. It now asks the addon once, reads from the debrid server directly, and asks again only if that address stops answering.
- The same converter reads about three times faster: 238 Mb/s instead of about 65 in a test with a 72 GB 4K remux. It used to open a new connection for every 2 MB it read. Busy scenes in large remuxes no longer buffer.

**Subtitles**
- Colour now has Silver, Grey and Soft yellow next to White, Yellow and Cyan (#8).
- A new Opacity setting, from 100% down to 50%, in Settings > Subtitles and in the player's subtitle menu.
