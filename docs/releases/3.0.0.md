A new top bar, a profile menu, and Shows, Movies and Anime tabs with rows of their own. Origin also moves to its own app id.

**Which file to download**
- Android phones and tablets: `io.github.betterstremio.origin_3.0.0_android-phone-beta.apk`
- Android TV, Google TV and most TV boxes: `io.github.betterstremio.origin_3.0.0_android-arm64.apk`
- Fire TV sticks and older boxes: `io.github.betterstremio.origin_3.0.0_android-armv7.apk` (`_android.apk` holds both CPU types)
- LG webOS: `io.github.betterstremio.origin_3.0.0_arm.ipk`
- Windows: `io.github.betterstremio.origin_3.0.0_windows_setup.exe`, or the portable zip
- Samsung TVs (preview): `io.github.betterstremio.origin_3.0.0_tizen-beta.tpk`

**A new app id**
- Origin now installs as `io.github.betterstremio.origin` instead of `tv.stremioorigin.app`, the id Stremio Origin uses. The two apps no longer replace each other and can sit on the same TV.
- Coming from 2.1.12 or older, the update installs the new app and opens it. On its first start it copies your sign-in, profiles and settings from the old app, then removes the old app and its torrent folder. Nothing to do on webOS and Windows. Android asks you once to confirm the removal.
- On Android, from 2.1.11 or older: update to 2.1.12 first, or install 3.0.0 by hand and sign in again.

**Samsung TVs (preview)**
- A first Samsung build, for Tizen 4 or later. We have not run it on a Samsung TV yet. It builds on Stremio Origin's Samsung port, which so far works best on 2018 to 2020 TVs (Tizen 4 to 5.5); newer TVs may close it during start-up.
- Install it with Apps2Samsung on a TV in Developer Mode. The app says when a new version is out, and you install that one the same way. Steps are in the [Samsung guide](https://github.com/betterstremio/origin/blob/main/docs/tizen.md).
- If it fails on your TV, open an issue with the TV model and its Tizen version.

**The top bar**
- Search, Home, Shows, Movies, Anime, Catalogue and My List, as plain words in the middle. The focus is a light pill that slides between them, and the page you are on has a grey one.
- Your profile picture sits on the left. The clock, the account name and the round buttons are gone.
- The red key still opens Settings and the green key still opens Search.

**The profile menu**
- Press Left from Search, or click your picture. A small card opens from the picture with your other profiles, Settings, Share logs and Exit.
- Choosing another profile switches to it straight away, or asks for its PIN first. "Switch profiles" opens the full chooser, where you can also add a profile.
- When an update is waiting, a dot shows on your picture and the card says so next to Settings.

**Shows and Movies**
- These replace Discover on TVs. Each opens on a billboard and has the same kind of rows as Home.
- Continue Watching of that kind comes first. With For You on, the next rows are picked for you: Watch again for films you finished in the last 60 days, Binge-worthy for series with at least two seasons, and more. Without it, the rows are Cinemeta's: Trending, New this year, Top rated, and one per genre.
- Rows load their titles only as you get near them.
- Phones keep Discover. The Classic Home layout keeps the old Discover look on TVs too.

**Anime**
- An Anime tab, off until you turn it on in Settings > Appearance. Its rows come from AniList, and titles open with Anime Kitsu's details and episodes. Anime Kitsu is added on this device only. Your Stremio or Nuvio account is not changed.
- With the tab on, anime leaves Shows and Movies. Series known only by their IMDb id, such as Cyberpunk: Edgerunners, are sorted into Anime too.

**Catalogue**
- A Catalogue tab lists your collections and every catalogue your addons offer. Turn it off in Settings > Appearance.

**Continue Watching**
- No limit on how many titles it shows.

**Start and sign-in**
- A new start animation: the Origin name, with its O drawn as a ring, on the same dark grey as Home. The start sound is gone.
- The sign-in screen, the first-run guide and What's new are plain and flat, without the glows.

**Appearance**
- Choosing an accent colour shows it on the Play button and progress bar as you move through the list. The accent now colours only highlights, Play and the progress bar. The top bar stays neutral.

**Pictures**
- Posters are kept compressed in memory and on disk, so far more of them stay loaded on a TV. On an LG C1 the app's peak memory fell from about 300 MB to about 150 MB, and cards coming back into view are ready more often.

**My List**
- Library is now My List: its name, the filters written out (All, Movies, Shows, In progress, Watched, Not watched), the sort, and your titles. The picture and details at the top are gone.

**Home**
- A new typeface, Onest, for every language except Arabic, which keeps its own. The app is also 690 KB smaller for it.
- Play and More info light up the same way the top bar does. Back from the rows returns to the billboard.
- Previews start past a trailer's rating card and studio logos, show no subtitles, and fill the billboard without black bars.
- A title whose logo is too dark to read shows its name instead.
- Continue Watching shows the time left beside the title, with a white progress line. The page background is a plain dark grey.
- Descriptions under a selected card are narrower and end with "…" when they are cut.
