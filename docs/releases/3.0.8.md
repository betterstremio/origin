With the Anime tab switched on (**Settings → Appearance**), anime show their English title logo instead of the Japanese one.

**What’s new**

- Anime cards, the Anime tab’s spotlight, title pages and the reviews panel use the English title logo from TMDB when metahub only has a Japanese one.
- If TMDB has only Japanese, Chinese or Korean logos for a title, Origin shows its English name as text.
- With the app in another language, anime use a logo in that language first, then the English one.
- Origin keeps TMDB’s answers on the device for three days, and title pages don’t wait for them.

**Which file to download**

- LG webOS uses `io.github.betterstremio.origin_3.0.8_arm.ipk` or the Homebrew Channel.
- Android TV, Google TV and most TV boxes use `io.github.betterstremio.origin_3.0.8_android-arm64.apk`.
- Fire TV sticks and older boxes use `io.github.betterstremio.origin_3.0.8_android-armv7.apk`. The `_android.apk` includes both CPU types.
- Android phones and tablets use `io.github.betterstremio.origin_3.0.8_android-phone-beta.apk`.
- Windows uses `io.github.betterstremio.origin_3.0.8_windows_setup.exe` or the portable zip.
- Samsung TVs (preview) use `io.github.betterstremio.origin_3.0.8_tizen-beta.tpk`.
- The Mac beta is available from the [3.0.2 release](https://github.com/betterstremio/origin/releases/tag/v3.0.2).
