Fixes for 4K remuxes with TrueHD or DTS sound on LG TVs, faster search, and a proper mouse on Windows.

**TV: TrueHD and DTS films**
- 4K remuxes whose sound has to be converted (Interstellar IMAX, Captain Marvel and other Dolby Vision hybrid copies) no longer show a broken picture in a small box at the top left. The picture plays full screen from the first second, after a seek and after resuming
- When a file has TrueHD plus an AC3 track, the TV now plays the AC3 track straight away instead of converting the sound
- If your audio language is only in a track the TV can't play (an English DTS track next to a Hindi AC3 one, say), that track is converted and you hear your language
- Converting the sound reads the file about four times faster, so big remuxes stop pausing to buffer
- Converted Dolby Vision files play as HDR10

**Updates**
- The update check on the TV no longer says "Could not check for updates" when GitHub's rate limit kicks in. It asks the website first

**Search**
- Results come sooner, and going back with Backspace shows earlier results at once
- The on-screen keyboard is QWERTY, on Search and on the sign-in screen
- Windows: no phone QR code on Search, type on your keyboard

**Windows**
- Moving the mouse no longer scrolls the page. Click a card that is partly off screen to bring it in
- Right-click a card for its details or streams, or use the Menu key or Shift+F10
- Arrows on the rows, a clickable billboard, scroll-wheel volume in the player, a draggable seek bar, and the controls hide over the picture after 2.5 s
- The layout fills any window or monitor shape (16:10, ultrawide) instead of showing bars. Thanks to vishaluchil
