A new player for Android phones and tablets. Double tap to skip works now, and the player has a screen lock, brightness and volume swipes, hold for 2× and pinch to zoom. TVs, Windows and Samsung are unchanged.

**Which file to download**
- Android phones and tablets: `io.github.betterstremio.origin_3.0.4_android-phone-beta.apk`
- Android TV, Google TV and most TV boxes: `io.github.betterstremio.origin_3.0.4_android-arm64.apk`
- Fire TV sticks and older boxes: `io.github.betterstremio.origin_3.0.4_android-armv7.apk` (`_android.apk` holds both CPU types)
- LG webOS: `io.github.betterstremio.origin_3.0.4_arm.ipk`
- Windows: `io.github.betterstremio.origin_3.0.4_windows_setup.exe`, or the portable zip
- Samsung TVs (preview): `io.github.betterstremio.origin_3.0.4_tizen-beta.tpk`
- Mac (beta): stays on 3.0.2 for now, from the [3.0.2 release](https://github.com/betterstremio/origin/releases/tag/v3.0.2)

**Double tap**
- A double tap on the left or right of the picture only flashed the controls and never skipped. Two things went wrong: the app measured the gap between taps in a way that missed most real double taps, and the controls shown by the first tap put the seek bar under the second one, so the film jumped to wherever your finger was. Both are fixed.
- Double tap the left or right third to go back or forward by your skip step (10 s unless you changed it in Settings). Each extra tap adds another step. The edge you tapped shows a ripple with the total.
- A single tap on the sides now waits about 0.3 s before showing the controls, in case a second tap follows. In the middle they show at once.

**New gestures**
- Swipe up or down on the left half for brightness, on the right half for volume. Brightness goes back to the phone's own setting when you close the player.
- Hold a finger on the picture to play at 2× until you let go.
- Pinch to change the picture size: Original, Fill, Zoom.
- A second finger cancels whatever the first one started.

**Layout**
- The controls are laid out for a phone: title and episode at the top, −10 / play / +10 in the middle, the bar with the time played and the time left at the bottom.
- Under the bar is a row of buttons: Speed, Lock, Episodes, Subtitles, Audio, picture size, and Next episode or Sources. When the row doesn't fit, buttons move into the ⋮ menu instead of having their names cut short.
- Speed steps through 1×, 1.25×, 1.5×, 2×, 0.5× and 0.75×, and stays the same when you switch to another source.
- Lock hides the controls and ignores touches. Tap once to show the "Screen locked" chip and tap the chip to unlock. Back shows the chip too, so a stray swipe doesn't close the film.
- Dragging the bar shows a bubble with the time and how far you moved.
- The controls keep clear of the camera cutout and the system's gesture area.
