Smoother browsing on 4K Android TVs. Phones, 1080p TVs and boxes, webOS, Windows and Samsung are unchanged.

**Which file to download**
- Android phones and tablets: `io.github.betterstremio.origin_3.0.5_android-phone-beta.apk`
- Android TV, Google TV and most TV boxes: `io.github.betterstremio.origin_3.0.5_android-arm64.apk`
- Fire TV sticks and older boxes: `io.github.betterstremio.origin_3.0.5_android-armv7.apk` (`_android.apk` holds both CPU types)
- LG webOS: `io.github.betterstremio.origin_3.0.5_arm.ipk`
- Windows: `io.github.betterstremio.origin_3.0.5_windows_setup.exe`, or the portable zip
- Samsung TVs (preview): `io.github.betterstremio.origin_3.0.5_tizen-beta.tpk`
- Mac (beta): stays on 3.0.2 for now, from the [3.0.2 release](https://github.com/betterstremio/origin/releases/tag/v3.0.2)

**4K Android TVs**
- On some 4K Google TVs, moving between posters on Home and My List dropped to about 2 frames a second while films played fine ([#19](https://github.com/betterstremio/origin/issues/19)). The app drew its menus at the panel's full 3840×2160, four times the pixels of 1080p, which is too much for the graphics chip in many TVs.
- Menus are now drawn at 1920×1080 and scaled up by the TV, the same as on LG webOS. Films still play at full 4K.
